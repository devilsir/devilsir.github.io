# Comidas, produtos e bancas dos mercados

Assets criados para VOICES com a ferramenta integrada **image_gen**, em 1º de novembro de 2026. As três folhas novas têm transparência real. A preparação para o jogo isolou as silhuetas de cada ícone para eliminar fragmentos de células vizinhas, mantendo transparência, amostragem por vizinho mais próximo e compactação WebP. Os arquivos finais estão nesta pasta; nenhuma referência do jogo depende da pasta pessoal de geração.

| Arquivo | Conteúdo | Grade final |
| --- | --- | --- |
| items-brazil.webp | Comidas, ingredientes, recipientes e artesanato | 4 × 4, células de 64 px |
| items-south-africa.webp | Comidas, bebidas, mercearia, tecidos e miçangas | 4 × 4, células de 64 px |
| market-stalls.webp | Oito fachadas de bancas e pequenas lojas | 4 × 2, células de 144 px |
| cape-facades.webp | Fachadas coloridas reutilizadas do pacote pixel art do Cabo já existente no jogo | Faixa de casas |

As embalagens das imagens são genéricas e simplificadas. Os nomes das marcas KOO e Three Cats / Da Gama aparecem no catálogo, com suas referências. São produtos sul-africanos; o jogo não apresenta essas marcas como empresas de propriedade negra. Os vendedores são personagens ficcionais.

Os valores de alimentação, hidratação, descanso e preços são regras da aventura, sem correspondência com medidas nutricionais ou preços reais.

## Prompts finais das três folhas geradas

### Comidas e objetos do Brasil

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D pixel-art game item sprite atlas. Create ONE square transparent sprite sheet, exactly 4 columns x 4 rows of equal-sized cells, 16 distinct isolated icons, ordered left to right then top to bottom. Every object centered and fully contained in its own cell with generous transparent padding; NO grid lines, NO text, NO labels, NO ground background, NO decorative extras. Actual crisp low-resolution pixel clusters, dark warm outlines 1 pixel, 3-tone shading, cozy top-down 3/4 RPG food/inventory art, earthy warm palette with teal bowls, golden browns, cream and muted green, matching a hand-pixelled 48px tile RPG. Acaraje is an opened golden brown black-eyed-pea fritter filled with orange vatapa, not a hamburger. Objects in row1: (1) acarajé with vatapá filling on a small leaf, (2) abará wrapped in a banana leaf opened to reveal ochre dumpling, (3) small bowl of feijão-fradinho with beans and greens, (4) white bowl of moqueca with tomato and herb colors. Row2: (5) yellow couscous of corn on teal plate, (6) two white-and-golden coconut cocada sweets, (7) bowl of white sweet corn mungunzá with cinnamon, (8) glass of purple grape/hibiscus juice. Row3: (9) reusable teal water flask with screw lid, (10) coconut water in green open coconut with simple straw, (11) woven fruit basket with bananas mango and orange, (12) brown paper bag of ingredients with green leafy vegetables. Row4: (13) folded multicolor Afro-Brazilian headwrap cloth in ochre green wine geometric pattern, (14) small brown woven handbasket with handles, (15) white ceramic bowl and wooden spoon, (16) bag of black-eyed peas with a small beans illustration and no writing. All 16 cells equally spaced in exact regular 4x4 layout. Transparency must be real alpha. No smooth illustration, no realistic photography, no lettering.

Exact regular grid, fully separate objects, centered each cell, transparent generous padding, no grid lines no text or labels. Row1 (1) plate of white maize pap with red-orange vegetable chakalaka beside it, (2) bowl of cracked maize and beans umngqusho, (3) two puffed golden round amagwinya fried breads, (4) two oval Cape Malay koesisters covered in coconut on teal plate, NOT braided koeksisters. ### Bancas e lojas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art game world prop atlas. Create ONE TRANSPARENT wide sprite sheet EXACTLY 4 columns x 2 rows of equal cells. Eight isolated shop/stall fronts for a cozy 2D top-down 3/4 orthographic RPG, each whole object centered with generous transparent margin inside own cell, feet all on same baseline in each row. No connecting ground or environment; NO people, NO text, NO labels, NO grid lines. Dark pixel outlines, crisp low resolution pixel clusters, earthy palette ochre teal wine cream green terracotta, NO soft airbrush or isometric diagonal. Front-facing game stall with small top visible and strong pixel shading. Row1: (1) Afro-Brazilian food stall of wood with green cream striped canvas awning, pots of acaraje and leaf wrapped food on counter; (2) fruit-and-water wooden cart with ochre canvas canopy, bananas green coconuts oranges, water dispenser; (3) artisan textile stall wine-red canopy with folded colorful headwrap fabrics and woven baskets displayed below; (4) small contemporary grocery kiosk dark green painted wood, cream flat sign blank, stacked simple tins water bottles sacks, tiny red hanging lantern. Row2: (5) Cape Town food market stall with teal-and-ochre awning, steaming iron cooking pot, white bowls and golden fried breads, brick counter; (6) Cape Town tea and spice stall cream awning with red edge, rooibos packets jars mugs small spices; (7) South African shweshwe textile and beadwork stall with ochre awning, indigo dotted cloth rolls, colorful small bracelets and baskets; (8) tiny Cape Town corner spaza shop front, warm ochre plaster and teal door frame, blank rectangular sign, boxed grocery stock and bottles on open counter. Every stall separate, no shared landscape, fit exact equal 4x2 grid. Alpha transparency, no shadows stretching across cells, no roof cropping. Integrate with SNES-like hand pixelled characters not a polished vector illustration.
